About me

I am Kshitij Jaggi. I live in Palo Alto and run RisaLabs, where I learn from the operations of care and cure.

This page is a list of the problems I believe are very, very, very important to solve in our lifetime. All the text here is mine. I will keep adding to it.

Mental health and basic wellbeing

Basic wellbeing is the foundation everything else stands on: sleep, movement, food, a calm mind.

A large part of suffering today is not disease in the clinical sense. It is people running on empty for years. Solving this is basic, and it is very very very important.

Drug discovery

We know more biology than ever before, but turning that knowledge into medicines is still slow, expensive, and uncertain.

The gap is rarely a lack of money or ideas. It is execution: the right people, the right experiments, run in the right order, without delay. Faster drug discovery means cures arrive inside a lifetime, not after it.

Delays in care

Care that arrives late arrives diminished. Approvals, authorizations, paperwork, and logistics stand between a patient and a treatment that already exists.

There should be no delay in care and cure. A system that makes a sick person wait for permission to get better is a problem we can solve in our lifetime.
